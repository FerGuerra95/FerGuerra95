# Pilot Outreach Execution Playbook

**Phase:** C.15.6  
**Purpose:** Execute the first 3-5 controlled pilot conversations without overstating product, legal, AI, or enterprise readiness.  
**Positioning:** Private Executive DSS / Board Intelligence Workspace.

---

## 1. Objective Of The First 3-5 Conversations

The goal is learning and qualification, not broad sales volume.

The first outreach round should answer:

- Who has urgent board-prep pain?
- Which segment understands DSS + human review fastest?
- Which buyer can provide minimum data safely?
- Which use case fits current product without heavy customization?
- Which prospects can proceed through NDA/DPA/SOW path if needed?

---

## 2. Ideal Profile

Prioritize:

1. family-owned private group;
2. private industrial group with board, M&A, funding, or compliance pressure;
3. family office;
4. M&A / corporate finance boutique;
5. industrial SME with reporting to owners, lenders, or partners.

Avoid first:

- large enterprise requiring formal procurement before any pilot;
- prospects requiring SOC2/ISO certification as a precondition;
- prospects asking for legal/investment decisions;
- prospects wanting autonomous AI approval.

---

## 3. How To Choose Targets

Pick targets with:

- one visible strategic decision;
- a likely board or owner reporting cadence;
- a warm path or credible relevance;
- manageable data sensitivity;
- a likely human reviewer;
- low-to-medium procurement friction.

Use `PILOT_TARGET_TRACKER_TEMPLATE.md` and score with `PILOT_GO_NO_GO_SCORECARD.md`.

---

## 4. How To Open The Conversation

Use a narrow problem statement:

> "We are piloting a private Board Intelligence workspace for teams that need to turn fragmented company data into review-ready Board Review Drafts with traceability and human review."

Then ask for context:

> "Is board pack preparation, acquisition review, funding narrative, or compliance/risk reporting painful right now?"

Do not ask for files in the first message.

---

## 5. How To Use IberNova

Use IberNova when:

- the prospect is curious but not ready to share data;
- the use case needs a concrete story;
- you need to demonstrate missing-data handling;
- production data is not available.

Say:

> "IberNova is fictional. It lets us show the workflow without exposing real client data."

Do not imply it is a real customer or real case.

---

## 6. How To Conduct Discovery

Use `PILOT_DISCOVERY_QUESTIONS.md`.

Keep discovery focused on:

- current board/reporting pain;
- decision deadline;
- existing data sources;
- human reviewer;
- legal/data sensitivity;
- pilot success criteria.

End with a go/no-go classification.

---

## 7. How To Propose A Demo

Offer a 25-35 minute demo:

1. CEO Overview.
2. Reporting / Board Packs.
3. Persisted snapshots.
4. HTML Board Review Draft.
5. Workflow reviewed/internal_final.
6. Optional M&A / Compliance / Funding deep dive.
7. Pilot offer.

Use synthetic data unless legal path is already approved.

---

## 8. How To Propose A Pilot

Propose a controlled pilot only if:

- sponsor exists;
- human reviewer exists;
- minimum data can be provided safely;
- the use case fits current product;
- legal path is realistic;
- the prospect accepts DSS limits.

Anchor the proposal around one review workflow, not all modules.

---

## 9. When To Ask For NDA

Ask for NDA before:

- receiving non-public business information;
- reviewing board materials;
- reviewing M&A, funding, supplier, compliance, or risk documents;
- seeing internal financials not already public.

Do not accept sensitive files casually by email or chat.

---

## 10. When To Ask For DPA / SOW

Ask for SOW before paid pilot work starts.

Assess DPA before:

- personal data is processed;
- client-controlled data is stored;
- pilot workspace is created for client data;
- subprocessor or provider AI questions arise.

---

## 11. What Not To Do

- Do not request real client data before legal path.
- Do not promise procurement readiness.
- Do not claim SOC2/ISO certification.
- Do not claim board-approved output.
- Do not sell autonomous AI.
- Do not provide legal or investment advice.
- Do not over-customize the first pilot.
- Do not hide missing data.

---

## 12. Red Flags

- "Can this approve the board pack?"
- "Can it replace counsel/advisors?"
- "Can we upload everything today before NDA?"
- "We need SOC2/ISO before discussion."
- "We need guaranteed funding/deal outcome."
- "We need broad integrations before pilot."
- "No human reviewer is available."

---

## 13. Learning Metrics

Track:

- reply rate;
- discovery booked;
- demo booked;
- use case clarity;
- legal friction;
- data availability;
- willingness to pay;
- segment fit;
- objections repeated across calls.

---

## 14. Success Criteria For The Round

The first round is successful if:

- 3-5 targeted conversations are started;
- at least 2 discovery calls are completed;
- at least 1 demo is scheduled or completed;
- at least 1 strong pilot candidate scores 40+;
- no false claims are introduced;
- no real data is collected outside legal path.
