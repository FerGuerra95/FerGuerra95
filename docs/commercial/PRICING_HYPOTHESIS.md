# CEO's OS Pricing Hypothesis

This is a pricing hypothesis, not final commercial policy.

## Controlled Pilot

**Price:** 3,000 EUR.

Includes:

- 1 use case.
- 1 private workspace.
- Limited data intake.
- Decision-support output.
- Human-reviewed pilot readout.

Best for validating one executive pain point quickly.

## Enterprise Pilot

**Price:** 7,500 EUR.

Includes:

- 2-3 modules.
- Weekly review.
- Board Review Draft.
- Data room or reporting draft where relevant.
- Pilot success criteria and final recommendation.

Best for validating cross-functional executive workflows.

## Strategic Pilot

**Price:** 15,000 EUR.

Includes:

- Multi-module pilot.
- CEO Overview.
- Reporting.
- M&A, Funding and Compliance combined where relevant.
- Executive review workshop.
- Expansion roadmap.

Best for strategic sponsors evaluating CEO's OS as a private operating workspace.

## Future subscription hypothesis

| Tier | Hypothesis | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Small private workspace | 1,500-3,000 EUR/month | Limited modules, private executive workspace. |
| Enterprise workspace | 5,000-10,000 EUR/month | Broader module coverage and operational cadence. |
| Custom strategic deployment | Custom | Requires procurement, legal, security and support roadmap. |

## Pricing guardrails

- Do not price as SOC2/ISO-certified SaaS.
- Do not price as SLA-backed procurement-ready enterprise software.
- Do not price as autonomous AI.
- Do not price as legal, compliance, investment or fairness opinion service.
- Do not price as a public marketplace or success-fee transaction platform.
