# CEO's OS One-Pager

## Headline

The private executive operating system for company intelligence.

## Definition

CEO's OS is a private executive decision-support system that helps leadership teams convert fragmented corporate information into traceable, reviewable and actionable decision signals.

CEO's OS is built for controlled executive pilots where every output remains DSS material, every important signal requires human review, and every board-facing artifact is treated as a Board Review Draft.

## The problem

Leadership teams often operate with:

- Fragmented corporate information across finance, legal, compliance, M&A, funding, governance and operating teams.
- Manual reporting cycles that make decisions slow and hard to trace.
- Board and executive decisions without a clear evidence trail.
- Compliance, funding and M&A workstreams that are disconnected from the CEO view.
- No single workspace for reviewing company intelligence before action.

## The solution

CEO's OS provides:

- A private executive workspace for corporate intelligence.
- DSS modules for operational decision-support.
- An Executive Overview that aggregates module signals without becoming the master operational store.
- Board Review Drafts for discussion, not automatic approval.
- Human-review workflows and truthfulness gates.
- Audit, backup and security pilot procedures documented for controlled use.

## Included modules

| Module | Pilot role |

| Funding | Rounds, runway, scenarios, dilution and readiness drafts. |
