# CEO's OS

## Private Executive Intelligence for Board-Level Decisions

**Subheadline:** A private decision-support workspace for M&A, Compliance, Funding and Executive Reporting — built around **traceable Board Review Drafts**, **persisted snapshots**, and **human review**.

*De datos dispersos a Board Review Drafts trazables.*

---

## What it is

**CEO's OS** is a **Private Executive DSS** and **Board Intelligence Workspace** for leadership teams who need to:

- Consolidate corporate signals without pretending one dashboard is the whole truth.  
- Prepare **Board Review Drafts** with clear labels and audit-friendly **persisted snapshots**.  
- Run a disciplined workflow (**draft → reviewed → internal_final**) with **human gates**.  
- Keep M&A, Compliance, and Funding workstreams connected to executive reporting.

It is **not** autonomous decision-making software.

---

## Who it is for

- CEOs, CFOs, and strategy/M&A leads preparing board or IC materials  
- Board secretaries and chiefs of staff who need **versioned, reviewable** packs  
- PE/portfolio operators running **controlled pilots** on portfolio companies  
- Teams that outgrew slides + spreadsheets but do not want false “certification” claims

## Board Intelligence layer

The commercial spine of CEO's OS:

1. **Create** Board Review Draft context from tenant-scoped DSS data.  
2. **Persist** a Board Review Snapshot (immutable renderer input).  
3. **Preview** with mandatory human-review labels.  
4. **Advance workflow** only through explicit human actions.  
5. **Optional (foundation):** AI Board Review Draft Assistant — **draft narrative only**.
