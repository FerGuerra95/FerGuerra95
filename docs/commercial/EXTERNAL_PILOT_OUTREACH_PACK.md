# External Pilot Outreach Pack

**Phase:** C.15.5  
**Positioning:** Private Executive DSS / Board Intelligence Workspace  
**Promise:** De datos dispersos a Board Review Drafts trazables, revisables y accionables.  
**Not:** Enterprise certified, procurement-ready, SOC2/ISO certified, autonomous AI, legal advice, investment advice, certified PDF, or board-approved output.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

CEO's OS is ready for 3-5 controlled pilot conversations with carefully selected prospects. The best early wedge is **Board Intelligence**: helping leadership teams convert fragmented M&A, funding, compliance, risk, and reporting signals into **Board Review Drafts** with persisted snapshots, audit metadata, and human review.

The outreach goal is not to sell a full enterprise platform. The goal is to secure a scoped pilot with a real sponsor, a named human reviewer, limited data intake, and clear success criteria.

- Wants automated legal/investment decisions.

## 7. 30-Second Pitch

CEO's OS is a private Board Intelligence workspace for leadership teams. It turns scattered M&A, funding, compliance, and reporting signals into traceable **Board Review Drafts** with persisted snapshots, audit metadata, and human review. It is decision support, not autonomous decision-making, and the pilot is designed to show whether your board-prep workflow can become faster and more disciplined in 4-6 weeks.

---

## 8. 2-Minute Pitch

Most leadership teams prepare board materials across spreadsheets, emails, dashboards, and static decks. That creates two problems: the materials take too long to assemble, and nobody has a clean record of what was shown, what was missing, and who reviewed it.

CEO's OS solves that as a **Private Executive DSS**. The Board Intelligence workflow starts with your operating signals, creates a **Board Review Draft**, persists the snapshot for traceability, and keeps human review gates visible. The product does not approve decisions, certify compliance, produce legal or investment advice, or claim board approval.

The pilot is intentionally narrow: one sponsor, one human reviewer, a minimum data intake, one board-style decision, and a review session. We can also use the fictional IberNova demo company first if you want to see the workflow before sharing data.

## 21. Legal Path Before Sensitive Data

Early outreach and first demos should use IberNova or other synthetic data. Do not ask prospects to send sensitive files in email or chat.

Before requesting or receiving non-public client data:

1. confirm whether the discussion is still exploratory or moving into pilot scope;
2. use the NDA path before sensitive confidential information is shared;
3. use the SOW path before paid pilot work starts;
4. assess whether a DPA is required before personal data or client-controlled data is processed;
5. define offboarding and deletion expectations before intake.

Use `docs/legal/PILOT_LEGAL_PACK.md` and `docs/legal/LEGAL_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md` as internal preparation only. They are drafts for professional review, not legally approved client documents.
