# Pilot Discovery Questions

**Use for:** first qualified conversation before a Board Intelligence pilot.  
**Goal:** determine whether a controlled pilot is useful, safe, and scoped.  
**Do not use for:** legal advice, investment advice, certified compliance, or board approval.

---

## 1. Company Context

- What type of company or portfolio are we discussing?
- Who owns the current board/reporting process?
- What is the current strategic priority?
- What is the reporting cadence?
- Which teams contribute inputs?

---

## 2. Critical Decisions

- What decision is coming in the next 30-90 days?
- Is it related to acquisition, funding, compliance, suppliers, risk, or governance?
- What would make the decision hard to prepare?
- What information is currently missing or disputed?

---

## 3. Reporting to Management / Board / Partners

- Who receives the current board or management pack?
- How long does it take to prepare?
- What usually changes in the last 48 hours?
- How do you track what was reviewed?
- What does "review-ready" mean internally?

---

## 4. M&A

- Are you evaluating a target, buyer, or strategic partnership?
- What stage is the process in?
- What assumptions are still unverified?
- What diligence questions keep returning?
- Do you need an IC-style draft, not a fairness opinion?

---

## 5. Funding / Debt / Capital

- Are you raising debt, equity, or internal capital approval?
- What funding amount and timeline are being discussed?
- What are the key assumptions: valuation, burn, runway, use of funds?
- Which data points are estimates?
- Who reviews investor/lender materials?

---

## 6. Compliance / Risk / Suppliers

- Are supplier, compliance, or risk gaps part of the decision?
- Do you have a supplier or risk register?
- Which evidence is available?
- Which evidence is missing?
- Who owns remediation?

---

## 7. Documentation Available

- Do you have a current board deck or agenda?
- Do you have a financial summary?
- Do you have module-level lists: suppliers, risks, targets, funding assumptions?
- What can be shared safely in a pilot?
- What must not be uploaded?

---

## 8. Traceability Pain

- Can you reconstruct what was shown in the last board pack?
- Can you see what was missing at the time?
- Can you tell who reviewed the material?
- Where do draft versions live?

---

## 9. Output Review

- Who must review outputs before circulation?
- What labels/disclaimers do you require?
- What would make a Board Review Draft useful?
- What would make it unsafe?

---

## 10. Decision Process

- Who is the economic buyer?
- Who is the daily owner?
- Who can approve a pilot?
- Who needs to be in the scoping call?

---

## 11. Urgency

- Is there a board date, IC date, financing date, or diligence milestone?
- What happens if the process stays manual?
- Is a 4-6 week pilot useful now?

---

## 12. Budget

- Is there budget for a controlled pilot?
- Is this owned by strategy, finance, transformation, M&A, compliance, or the CEO office?
- Is EUR 7.5k-12k for a focused Board Intelligence Pilot in range?

---

## 13. Success Criteria

- What would prove value in 4-6 weeks?
- Time saved?
- Better review discipline?
- Clearer missing-data map?
- Better board questions?
- Reusable reporting workflow?

---

## 14. Legal / Compliance Risks

- Are there regulated data constraints?
- Is special category personal data involved?
- Is legal privilege involved?
- Do we need NDA/DPA before data sharing?
- Who approves data transfer?

---

## 15. Data They Can / Cannot Share

Can share:

- company profile;
- management summary;
- high-level board objective;
- module-specific CSVs;
- metadata-first evidence lists.

Cannot share without review:

- secrets;
- full production DB dumps;
- raw customer PII;
- privileged legal advice;
- highly sensitive HR or regulated personal data.

---

## Go / No-Go After Discovery

| Decision | Criteria | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Go | Pain real, sponsor/reviewer clear, data safe, scope narrow | Schedule demo/scoping |
| Go with conditions | Pain real but data/legal path incomplete | Send intake checklist and legal prerequisites |
| Nurture | Interest exists but no urgency | Send one-pager and revisit later |
| No-go | Wants certification, autonomous decisions, or unsafe data use | Decline or defer |
