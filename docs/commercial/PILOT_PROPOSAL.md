# CEO's OS Executive Intelligence Pilot

## Pilot name

CEO's OS Executive Intelligence Pilot.

## Suggested duration

4-6 weeks.

## Suggested pricing options

| Option | Price | Best fit |
|---|---:|---|
| Option A - Controlled Pilot | 3,000 EUR | 1 focused use case, limited data, fast validation. |
| Option B - Enterprise Pilot | 7,500 EUR | 2-3 modules, weekly review, board review draft. |
| Option C - Strategic Pilot | 15,000 EUR | Multi-module executive review with workshop. |

Prices are pilot hypotheses and not final commercial policy.

## Scope

The pilot evaluates CEO's OS as a private executive DSS for leadership preparation and operational decision-support. Outputs are indicative, traceable and human-reviewed.

Recommended pilot scope:

- 1 private workspace.
- Named pilot sponsor and named human review owner.
- Limited, approved business data.
- Weekly review cadence.
- Final pilot readout with next-step recommendation.

## Modules included

Choose based on pilot option:

- Executive Overview.
- M&A.
- Compliance.
- Funding.
- PMI.
- Risk.
- Reporting.
- Governance.
- Strategy.

Bridge internal signals may be referenced only as internal/unlisted DSS signals. Heritage should be treated as preview/future unless separately scoped.

## Data needed

- Company profile and module selection.
- Approved pilot users and roles.
- Limited sample records for chosen modules.
- Redacted or synthetic documents where possible.
- Success criteria and target decisions to support.

Do not include secrets, production customer database copies, special category data, payment card data, government ID bulk data, or material not covered by NDA/pilot agreement.

## Exclusions

This pilot does not include:

- Formal SLA.
- SOC2/ISO certification.
- Final legal-reviewed DPA or privacy policy.
- Procurement-ready security questionnaire.
- Autonomous AI agents.
- Public marketplace.
- Verified buyer network.
- Success-fee transaction layer.
- Legal advice, investment advice, fairness opinion or certified compliance audit.
- Complete PDF renderer unless a separate renderer phase is authorized.

## Deliverables

- Configured controlled pilot workspace.
- Module walkthrough and training session.
- Weekly pilot review notes.
- Board Review Draft or executive review draft when relevant.
- Pilot issue log and open limitation register.
- Final pilot summary with expansion recommendation.

## Human review

Every output is DSS material and requires human review before board, legal, investment, compliance or external use. The customer must assign a named human review owner.

## Success criteria

Pilot success should be measured by:

- Time saved in preparing executive review material.
- Better traceability from signal to source.
- Clearer prioritization across selected modules.
- No open P0/P1 at pilot close.
- Human reviewers find Board Review Drafts useful and appropriately limited.
- Security/privacy operating rules followed.

## Security posture

Security and privacy pilot procedures are documented, including access review, credential hygiene, audit logging for key actions, backup/restore procedure and secure-share guidelines. This is pilot readiness, not SOC2/ISO certification or procurement readiness.

## Legal and compliance limits

CEO's OS does not provide legal advice, investment advice, tax advice, fairness opinions, certified compliance audits, board approvals or guaranteed outcomes. Final external deployment requires legal review of customer-facing documents, DPA/privacy materials and data processing terms.

## Offboarding

At pilot end:

- Export agreed artifacts.
- Review open issues and success criteria.
- Revoke pilot users and secure shares.
- Rotate credentials if needed.
- Backup final state if required.
- Delete or retain data per agreement; retention/DSR automation remains future work.

## Next step after pilot

Options after pilot:

- Stop and offboard.
- Extend pilot to additional modules.
- Move to enterprise procurement preparation.
- Define a production hardening roadmap covering legal review, retention/DSR, SLA/support, SOC2/ISO roadmap, security questionnaire, audit export, SSO/SAML/SCIM roadmap and DR/BCP.
