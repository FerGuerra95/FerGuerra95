# C.24.1 - Visual Academy Plan

## Purpose

Create a controlled visual education layer for CEO's OS / The Sovereign OS so prospects, pilots, and internal reviewers can understand the product without receiving unrestricted access to every branch.

This plan is documentation only. It does not create videos, upload heavy assets, generate real founder imagery, enable AI runtime, or change product behavior.

## Positioning

CEO's OS should be explained as:

- Private Executive DSS.
- Board Intelligence Workspace.
- Human-reviewed operating layer.
- Synthetic-first demo environment.
- Board Review Draft workflow, not board-approved output.

Do not position CEO's OS as:

- Certified enterprise platform.
- Procurement-ready platform.
- SOC2/ISO certified system.
- Legal or investment advisor.
- Fairness opinion engine.
- Certified compliance audit system.
- Autonomous AI decision-maker.
- Public marketplace.
- Provider AI runtime in production.

## Flagship Onboarding Asset

The practical user manual video is the flagship onboarding asset for qualified pilots. It should be sent after discovery or used during guided onboarding, not as a cold first-touch asset.

The video should show:

- Login and app navigation.
- CEO Overview as aggregator.
- Reporting / Board Packs.
- Persisted snapshot creation.
- HTML Board Review Draft preview.
- Workflow states.
- Browser-native print/save as PDF.
- Limited branch walkthroughs.

It must not imply that CEO's OS produces a certified PDF, approves board materials, gives legal/investment advice, or uses production AI provider traffic.

## Production Workflow

1. Select the video scope and branch.
2. Confirm whether the video is public, unlisted, or internal.
3. Use IberNova or another synthetic dataset only.
4. Capture clean screenshots or short screen recordings with no secrets, real client data, tokens, cookies, or personal data.
5. Write script from `BRANCH_VIDEO_SCRIPT_INDEX.md`.
6. Review against `VIDEO_TRUTHFULNESS_CHECKLIST.md`.
7. If using a founder avatar/image, follow `AI_AVATAR_AND_FOUNDER_IMAGE_GUIDELINES.md`.
8. Render a lightweight draft outside the repository.
9. Store final video externally; keep only scripts/checklists in repo.
10. Version the video in `VIDEO_HOSTING_AND_VERSIONING.md`.

## Screenshot Rules

- Use IberNova Industrial Group S.L. or clearly synthetic records.
- Hide browser extensions, local file paths, tokens, cookies, auth headers, and debug consoles.
- Do not include real target names, prospect names, emails, or call notes.
- Keep labels visible: Board Review Draft, Human Review Required, Not Board Approved, Confidential, Not Legal Advice, Not Investment Advice.
- Keep missing data visible as N/A, insufficient_data, not provided, or board-review questions.

## Review Gates

Every visual asset must pass:

- Product truthfulness review.
- Security/privacy review.
- DSS and human-review language review.
- Claims review.
- Synthetic-data review.
- Versioning review.

## Publishing Decision

| Decision | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Publish public/unlisted | Synthetic data only, no unsafe claims, no secrets, no real client data |
| Keep internal | Shows unfinished/future module, needs operator explanation, or includes sensitive process context |
| Block | Contains real data, unsafe claims, hidden missing data, provider AI implication, or unapproved founder image |
