# AI Avatar and Founder Image Guidelines

## Purpose

Define safe rules for using an AI-generated avatar, likeness, or founder image for Fernando in CEO's OS visual academy materials.

This document does not authorize use of a real image. It defines the consent and review steps required before any avatar/image is published.

## Consent Rules

- Do not use a real photo of Fernando without explicit written approval.
- Do not generate a likeness intended to impersonate Fernando without explicit written approval.
- Do not upload real photos to a third-party generator unless the tool, retention policy, and consent path are approved.
- Do not use client, prospect, employee, or third-party likenesses.
- Do not imply the avatar is live, autonomous, or speaking unscripted.

## Allowed Uses

| Use | Status | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Stylized founder avatar | Allowed after consent | Must be labelled as illustrative or AI-generated if materially synthetic |
| Voiceover by Fernando | Allowed after consent | Script must pass truthfulness checklist |
| Still image in intro/outro | Allowed after consent | No fake endorsement or certification implication |
| Animated avatar | Restricted | Must not imply autonomous AI or real-time advisor |

## Prohibited Uses

- Deepfake-like impersonation without explicit consent.
- Fake customer testimonial.
- Fake board, regulator, auditor, investor, or legal endorsement.
- Avatar claiming legal approval, investment advice, certification, SOC2/ISO status, board approval, or provider AI production traffic.
- Real client data or confidential screenshots in avatar generation prompts.

## Prompting Guidelines

Use neutral founder imagery prompts such as:

> "Professional founder portrait for a private executive decision-support software introduction, neutral studio background, realistic but clearly produced visual, sober enterprise tone."

Avoid:

- "Official certified enterprise advisor."
- "Regulator-approved."
- "Board-approved AI."
- "Legal or investment expert giving advice."

## Review Checklist

- [ ] Consent recorded.
- [ ] Image source approved.
- [ ] Generator/tool reviewed for retention and reuse policy.
- [ ] No real client/prospect data used.
- [ ] Script reviewed for DSS/human-review wording.
- [ ] No certification or autonomous-AI claim.
- [ ] Version logged in hosting/versioning register.

## Default Decision

Until explicit consent and tool review are completed, use a non-likeness brand visual or product screenshot instead of a founder image.
