# Founder Avatar Production Plan

## 1. Purpose

Define the safe path for using an AI-generated founder image, avatar, voice, or presenter in CEO's OS Visual Academy materials.

## 2. Required Fernando Approval

Any AI-generated founder image, avatar, voice, or presenter must be approved by Fernando before external use.

Approval must cover:

- Source image.
- Voice source or synthetic voice.
- Script.
- Tool/provider.
- Output version.
- Intended audience.

## 3. Source Image Requirements

- Use only approved images.
- Do not use private, scraped, or third-party images.
- Do not upload source images to tools without tool/privacy review.
- Do not include clients, prospects, or third parties.

## 4. Voice Requirements

- Use approved voice recording or approved synthetic voice.
- Do not clone or imitate voice without explicit approval.
- Review pronunciation of CEO's OS, IberNova, Board Review Draft, and Human Review Required.

## 5. Script Approval

Scripts must pass:

- DSS positioning.
- Human review wording.
- No board-approved claim.
- No certified PDF claim.
- No autonomous AI claim.
- No legal/investment advice claim.
- No SOC2/ISO claim.

## 6. Where Avatar Can Be Used

- Short intro.
- Closing CTA.
- Founder context section.
- Internal training.

## 7. Where Avatar Cannot Be Used

- Fake testimonials.
- Fake client endorsements.
- Legal, investment, audit, compliance, or board approval statements.
- Claims of certification or procurement readiness.
- Unreviewed real-client contexts.

## 8. Disclosure Guidelines

Where appropriate, label the avatar or voice as AI-generated or produced visual media. Do not imply the avatar is a live autonomous assistant.

## 9. No Fake Testimonial Rule

The avatar must not present itself as a client, investor, board member, regulator, auditor, lawyer, or independent certifier.

## 10. No Unapproved Claims

Avatar scripts inherit all academy truthfulness rules.

## 11. No Impersonation Without Review

No likeness, voice clone, or presenter can be used externally without explicit review and approval.

## 12. Versioning

Use the versioning policy:

`CEOS_VisualAcademy_FounderAvatar_{AssetName}_v{version}_YYYY-MM-DD`

## 13. External Storage

Store avatar source/export files outside the repository in an approved controlled folder.

## 14. Delete / Revoke Old Versions

Revoke or archive old avatar versions when:

- Script changes materially.
- Product claims change.
- Consent is withdrawn.
- Tool/provider status changes.
- A claim or visual risk is discovered.
