# Legal Review Checklist

**Status:** Internal checklist for professional legal review.  
**Not:** Legal advice or confirmation of legal readiness.

---

## 1. NDA Review

- Parties and legal names correct.
- Confidential information definition appropriate.
- Exclusions complete.
- Term acceptable.
- return/deletion clause aligned with pilot.
- governing law and venue completed.

---

## 2. DPA Review

- Controller/processor roles assessed.
- GDPR/RGPD applicability assessed.
- Processing details complete.
- personal data categories minimized.
- security measures acceptable.
- deletion/return process defined.

---

## 3. SOW Review

- Scope is narrow.
- modules included are explicit.
- deliverables are review drafts, not final advice.
- out-of-scope section is clear.
- fees, payment, tax, and termination are reviewed.

---

## 4. Privacy Notice Alignment

- Confirm whether pilot processing is covered by client notices.
- Confirm whether CEO's OS provider needs separate notice language.
- Confirm data subject request path.

---

## 5. Subprocessor Review

- List all subprocessors.
- Confirm location and purpose.
- Confirm contractual flow-down requirements.
- Confirm approval process.

---

## 6. AI Provider / Subprocessor Review

- Confirm no production provider AI traffic before approval.
- Confirm whether AI provider is a subprocessor.
- Confirm transfer mechanism.
- Confirm data retention controls.
- Confirm prompt/output handling.

---

## 7. Liability Cap

- Define cap.
- Define exclusions.
- Confirm no reliance for regulated advice.
- Confirm customer responsibility for final decisions.

---

## 8. DSS Disclaimer

- CEO's OS is DSS.
- Outputs require human review.
- No autonomous decisions.
- No board-approved output.

---

## 9. No Legal / Investment Advice

- Confirm all templates and commercial docs avoid legal advice.
- Confirm no investment recommendation.
- Confirm no fairness opinion.
- Confirm no certified valuation.

---

## 10. No Certification Claims

- No certified compliance claim.
- No SOC2/ISO certification claim.
- No procurement readiness claim.
- No certified PDF claim.

---

## 11. Data Retention

- Retention period defined.
- backup lifecycle defined.
- audit log retention defined.
- deletion exceptions documented.

---

## 12. Offboarding

- data export/return defined.
- deletion evidence defined.
- account disablement defined.
- retained logs explained.

---

## 13. Jurisdiction

- governing law completed.
- venue completed.
- consumer/SME/enterprise status checked.
- cross-border implications checked.

---

## 14. Billing / Tax

- fee terms reviewed.
- VAT/tax handling reviewed.
- expenses reviewed.
- cancellation terms reviewed.

---

## 15. Insurance

- confirm whether professional liability, cyber, or tech E&O insurance is required.
- do not claim coverage unless confirmed.

---

## 16. Real Estate / Funding / Transaction Layer Caution

If future workflows involve transactions, capital raising, lending, real estate, buyer matching, or introductions, counsel must assess regulated activity risk before launch.

---

## 17. Success Fee Caution

Do not offer success fees, brokerage, placement, deal matching, or regulated transaction compensation without legal review.

---

## 18. KYC / AML Caution

If any future transaction layer activates, KYC/AML obligations must be assessed before use.
---

## 19. AI Provider Runtime Review

- provider terms reviewed;
- DPA/subprocessor status approved;
- no-training/default retention reviewed;
- prompt/output retention reviewed;
- international transfer mechanism reviewed if applicable;
- client approval path defined;
- data classes approved/excluded;
- redaction/minimization controls reviewed;
- prompt-injection risk reviewed;
- kill switch and incident process reviewed.

No real client data should be sent to an AI provider until this review is complete.
