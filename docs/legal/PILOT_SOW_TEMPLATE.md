## 3. Scope

The pilot will evaluate CEO's OS as a Private Executive DSS / Board Intelligence Workspace for a limited business decision or board-preparation workflow.

Pilot scope:

- controlled data intake;
- Board Intelligence workspace;
- Reporting / Board Packs;
- persisted Board Review Snapshot workflow;
- human review session;
- missing-data map.

---

## 4. Modules Included

| Module | Included? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Board Intelligence | Yes | Core pilot |
| Reporting | Yes | Board Review Draft and snapshots |
| M&A | Optional | DSS only; not fairness opinion |
| Compliance | Optional | Operational support; not certified compliance |
| Funding | Optional | Scenario support; not investment advice |

---

## 5. Deliverables

- Board Review Draft.
- Persisted snapshot / report pack.
- Review session with named human reviewer.
- Findings summary.
- Missing-data map.
- Board-review questions.
- Pilot retrospective against success criteria.

All deliverables are DSS materials requiring human review.

---

## 6. Out Of Scope

- Legal advice.
- Investment advice.
- Fairness opinion.
- Certified compliance.
- Board approval.
- Certified PDF.
- Autonomous AI.
- External integrations unless agreed in writing.
- Production AI provider traffic unless DPA/subprocessor approval is completed.
- Public marketplace or transaction execution.
- Success-fee brokerage or regulated transaction advice.

---

## 8. Client Responsibilities

The client will:

- appoint a sponsor and human reviewer;
- provide authorized data only;
- avoid prohibited data unless separately approved;
- review outputs before circulation;
- make final business, legal, compliance, and investment decisions outside CEO's OS;
- confirm offboarding requirements.

---

## 9. CEO's OS Responsibilities

CEO's OS provider will:

- operate the pilot workspace within agreed scope;
- preserve DSS/human-review labels;
- show missing data transparently;
- avoid certification or approval claims;
- support data handling and offboarding according to the SOW/DPA;
- provide pilot findings summary.

## 11. Human Review Process

Outputs remain Board Review Drafts until reviewed by the client-designated human reviewer. `reviewed` and `internal_final` are workflow metadata only and do not mean board-approved.

## 12. Success Criteria

Example criteria:

- minimum data package processed without prohibited data;
- at least one Board Review Draft produced;
- missing-data map reviewed;
- human reviewer completes review session;
- no DSS/truthfulness violations;
- pilot sponsor decides continue/hold/stop.
