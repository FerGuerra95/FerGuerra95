## 2. Purpose

The parties wish to discuss a potential controlled pilot of CEO's OS, a private executive decision-support workspace. Confidential Information may be shared only for evaluating, scoping, and operating that pilot.

---

## 3. Confidential Information

Confidential Information may include non-public:

- business plans;
- financial information;
- strategy, board, or management materials;
- M&A, funding, compliance, supplier, risk, or reporting information;
- product information;
- pilot data and outputs;
- technical or security information.

Confidential Information includes information disclosed in writing, orally, visually, electronically, or through controlled pilot access.

---

## 4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the receiving party can show:

- is public through no breach of this Agreement;
- was already lawfully known;
- was independently developed without use of Confidential Information;
- was lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality duty;
- must be disclosed by law, court, or regulator, subject to notice where legally permitted.

---

## 5. Obligations

The receiving party must:

- use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
- protect it with reasonable care;
- restrict access to personnel and advisors with a need to know;
- ensure such personnel and advisors are bound by confidentiality obligations;
- not disclose it except as permitted by this Agreement.

---

## 6. Permitted Use

Confidential Information may be used only to evaluate, scope, price, prepare, operate, or review the controlled pilot.

CEO's OS outputs remain decision-support drafts requiring human review. They are not legal advice, investment advice, compliance certification, fairness opinions, board-approved outputs, or certified PDFs.

---

## 7. No Disclosure

Neither party may disclose the other party's Confidential Information to third parties except:

- professional advisors under confidentiality duties;
- approved subcontractors/subprocessors if separately authorized;
- as required by law.

---

- backup copies deleted in ordinary course;
