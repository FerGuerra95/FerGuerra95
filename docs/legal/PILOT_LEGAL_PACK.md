# Pilot Legal Pack

**Phase:** C.19.0  
**Status:** Internal operational draft for legal review  
**Product posture:** Private Executive DSS / Board Intelligence Workspace / Human Review Required

> This document is an internal operational draft and must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before being used with clients.

---

## 1. Purpose

This pack defines the legal-operations path for external pilot conversations and paid controlled pilots. It helps the team know when to use synthetic data, when to require NDA/DPA/SOW review, and how to preserve CEO's OS truthfulness limits.

It is not legal advice and is not a substitute for professional review.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to:

- external pilot discovery;
- synthetic-data demos using IberNova Industrial Group S.L.;
- controlled Board Intelligence pilots;
- limited client data intake;
- Board Review Draft preparation;
- pilot offboarding.

Does not apply to:

- production enterprise procurement;
- regulated legal, investment, audit, or compliance certification services;
- public secure-share rollout;
- provider AI runtime with client data unless DPA/subprocessor path is approved;
- success-fee transaction operations.

---

## 3. When To Use This Pack

Use this pack when:

- a prospect wants to share non-public company information;
- a pilot may process client data or personal data;
- a paid pilot SOW is being prepared;
- a customer asks about confidentiality, DPA, deletion, or subprocessor handling;
- a demo may move from IberNova/synthetic data to real client data.

---

## 4. When Not To Use It

Do not use this pack as:

- a signed legal agreement;
- a final DPA;
- a final NDA;
- a final SOW;
- a privacy policy;
- evidence of SOC2/ISO certification;
- procurement approval.

---

| Pilot SOW | Before any paid pilot or client-specific work | Draft template; legal review required |

| Security/trust appendix | If requested by client or needed for pilot risk | Separate review; not certification |

---

## 6. Pilot Legal Workflow

1. Exploratory call: no confidential data requested.
2. Demo: use IberNova or other synthetic data.
3. Discovery: identify scope, human reviewer, modules, and data categories.
4. NDA: execute before sensitive data exchange.
5. SOW/DPA: approve before real pilot processing where required.
6. Data intake: minimize data, label missing data, avoid prohibited uploads.
7. Human review: all Board Review Drafts remain review material.
8. Offboarding: export agreed deliverables, delete or return data per contract, log completion.

---

## 7. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| CEO's OS provider | Operates pilot workspace and DSS workflow within agreed scope |
| Client | Provides authorized data, reviewer, and business context |
| Human reviewer | Reviews Board Review Drafts and decides what can circulate internally |
| Data owner | Confirms data categories, sensitivity, and offboarding requirements |
| Legal reviewer | Reviews NDA, DPA, SOW, liability, jurisdiction, and data terms |

---

## 8. DSS Limitation

CEO's OS is a Decision Support System. It does not replace directors, officers, lawyers, auditors, compliance officers, investment advisors, banks, or committees. Outputs are Board Review Drafts and pilot deliverables for human review, not final decisions.

---

## 9. AI Limitation

AI may be planned or used only as draft-assistant support after the approved provider/runtime/legal path. AI must not:

- approve reports;
- mark reviewed or internal-final;
- provide legal or investment advice;
- certify compliance;
- recalculate official scores;
- process real client data without DPA/subprocessor approval.

---

## 10. Data Limitation

Before NDA/DPA/SOW approval, use synthetic data. When real data is approved:

- collect minimum necessary data;
- avoid secrets, credentials, raw tokens, raw DB dumps, and unnecessary personal data;
- label missing or uncertain data;
- convert missing data into review questions;
- do not convert missing values into zero.

---

## 11. Liability Limitation Principles

Legal counsel should review:

- pilot-only scope;
- no reliance for legal/investment decisions;
- no certification or guaranteed outcomes;
- limitation of liability;
- exclusions for indirect damages;
- customer responsibility for final decisions;
- disclaimers for third-party data quality and customer-provided information.

---

## 12. External Legal Review Checklist

Before using with clients, counsel should review:

- NDA terms;
- DPA roles and processing details;
- SOW scope and exclusions;
- privacy notice alignment;
- subprocessor list;
- security measures;
- retention and deletion;
- liability cap;
- governing law;
- tax/billing terms;
- any regulated transaction implications.

---

## 13. Go / No-Go Before Pilot

Go only if:

- sponsor and human reviewer are named;
- scope is narrow;
- data categories are understood;
- NDA/DPA/SOW path is agreed where required;
- no prohibited claims are needed to close the pilot;
- offboarding is defined.

No-go if:

- prospect asks for autonomous decision-making;
- prospect needs legal, investment, or compliance certification;
- real data is requested before legal path;
- client requires SOC2/ISO certification as a condition;
- the pilot would require provider AI traffic before DPA/subprocessor approval.
